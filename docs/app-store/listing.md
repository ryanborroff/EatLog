## Description (4000 chars max)
```
Say what you ate. We'll do the maths.

EatLog is a voice-first food diary. Tap the mic, describe what you're
eating in your own words, and EatLog works out the calories and
macros — no searching a food database, no manual quantity entry.

"Two scrambled eggs, two slices of sourdough with butter and a large
flat white." Logged in seconds.

HOW IT WORKS
• Tap the mic and speak naturally — or type if you'd rather
• EatLog identifies each food, estimates quantities, and calculates
  calories, protein, carbs and fat
• Everything is added to the right meal automatically
• Say "actually it was tuna" or "add mayonnaise" to correct an entry —
  no need to delete and re-log

BUILT FOR SPEED, NOT PERFECTION
EatLog estimates are clearly marked as estimates. The goal isn't
laboratory precision — it's consistent tracking that doesn't feel like
a chore. A reasonable estimate logged every day beats a perfect number
you gave up entering weeks ago.

FEATURES
• Voice and text food logging
• Automatic meal categorisation (breakfast, lunch, dinner, snack)
• Daily calorie and macro tracking against your own targets
• Personal foods and "usual" shortcuts — save your regular meals and
  log them by name
• Conversational corrections
• Ask EatLog questions about your own diary ("how much protein have I
  had today?")
• Weekly history and simple trends

EatLog is a personal tracking tool, not a medical device. Nutrition
values are estimates and should not be relied on for clinical or
medical decisions.
```
